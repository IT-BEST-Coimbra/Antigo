External Events

Our External Events are open to all students of the Faculty of Sciences and Technology of the University of Coimbra. With this type of events we seek to promote students through national and international opportunities.

BEST Engineering Week
27th to 30th November
Three days where we bring to Coimbra a chance of networking between students and companies, through company stands, workshops and many more activities.
RESET
2th to 3th March
We challenge around 80 students to combine their hard skills and their creativity to create, in 24 hours, an innovative solution to a real problem proposed by a company.
BEST Course
17th to 27th July
BEST organizes international courses where international students of technology get the chance to complement their knowledge in different fields of study. BEST Coimbra organizes a course in our city, where we welcome around 24 students to our University.
Internal Events

Events, directed for BEST members, that aim to aid member's self development and improve networking between members.

Motivational Weekend
Every semester we organise a weekend full of learning opportunities, soft skill development and bonding time. This event has the purpose of creating a better network between the members and introduce new recruits to the BEST spirit.
Training Weekend
One of our objectives as an organisation is to promote the evolution of our members. To better development of our members, we organise a weekend filled with training sessions on soft and hard skills facilitated by certified members.
International Events
From sharing and comparing our working methods with other local groups in Regional Meetings, to learning about different cultures in Cultural Exchanges, the opportunities for BEST members at an international level are endless and extremely enriching.
