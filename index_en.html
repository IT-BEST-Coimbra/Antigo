What is BEST?

BEST stands for Board of European Students of Technology. We are a non-profit

international organisation dedicated to promoting cross-cultural communication and

exchange among European students of technology.

What is BEST Coimbra

BEST Coimbra is the local BEST group of the University of Coimbra. Our mission

is to develop and dynamize the students of the Faculty of Sciences and

Technology of the University of Coimbra, through a range of diverse events.

Why become a member of BEST

Being a BEST member is unlike any other experience and it opens doors to many unique opportunities:

  • 90% of BEST members get a job right after finishing University;
  • BEST members are presented with travelling opportunities almost weekly;
  • Members have access to a range of essential training sessions that prepare us for the working world.
