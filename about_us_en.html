Who we are?

BEST stands for Board of European Students of Technology and we are a non-profit international organisation dedicated to promoting cross-cultural communication among European students.

Since 1989, BEST provides communication, cooperation and exchange opportunities. Our goal is to help students become globally-minded by fostering a deeper understanding of different European cultures.

Our Vision

BEST helps students to achieve an international mindset, reach a better understanding of cultures, and develop the capacity to work in culturally diverse environments.

We create opportunities for personal development of students and support them in reaching their full potential.

Our Mission

Developing students: we provide events and services to empower them with the skills and knowledge to succeed in a global environment.

We aim to deliver educational involvement, career support, and a dynamic, engaging community.
