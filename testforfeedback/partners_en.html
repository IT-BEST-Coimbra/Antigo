OUR RESULTS

As a student organisation who works closely with the university we have a lot to offer in terms of promotion next to the student community.

As BEST Coimbra we reach about 500 technology students through our events every year.

This year, we had 80 participants from the Faculty of Science and Technology of the University of Coimbra in our Engineering Competition. These participants came from all areas of knowledge, since students of Computer Science and Design and Multimedia to Chemical Engineering and Physics.

In 2023 we also organised our BEST Course in Summer that counted with 24 participants. This event occurred between the dates of July 19th-28th and it worked the themes of 3D printing and additive manufacturing.

OUR SERVICES

Workshops
Brief intensive educational sessions that are focused on certain topics or skills. Through these, students are better prepared in soft and hard skills.
Job fairs
These events are designed to develop students' web of connections facilitating the contact with companies fostering meaningful interactions and creating valuable networking opportunities for both parties.
Case studies
Students are presented with a real problem or challenge proposed by companies, that they have to solve using their creativity skills.
